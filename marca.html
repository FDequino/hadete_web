What is printed, and why

Four things appear on every front label. They are the four a buyer asks for when a lot is questioned, so they travel with the jar rather than with the paperwork.

  1. Region, not countrySan Juan, Salta or Cuyo, because "Argentina" says nothing about the climate the fruit grew in.
  2. Harvest monthNot just a best-before date. For a southern-hemisphere crop, the harvest month is what tells you how fresh the lot is.
  3. Lot numberThe same number on the certificate of analysis and the transaction certificate, so a jar on a shelf can be traced back to a plant.
  4. What was not addedNo added sugar, no sulphites, cold extracted. Stated plainly instead of implied with a leaf.
